GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

Here are a few basics to review before exploring any rewards program.

Contact us →
Is GiftCash a gift card issuer?

GiftCash is a rewards information landing page, not a gift card issuer. Any third-party offer is governed by that provider's terms.

Will I definitely receive a gift card?

No. Participation does not guarantee a reward. Eligibility, completion rules, verification, availability, and other conditions are set by each offer provider.

Who can participate in an offer?

Requirements vary by program and may include location, age, device, or account restrictions. Review the official terms before participating.

How do I protect my personal information?

Review the provider's privacy policy, use unique passwords, and do not share account passwords or sensitive information unless you understand why it is required and trust the provider.

Does GiftCash operate third-party offers?

Third-party providers may operate their own offers and set their own requirements. GiftCash does not control their eligibility decisions or reward fulfillment.
